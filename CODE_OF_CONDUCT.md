# Code of Conduct

Tinlance adopts the Contributor Covenant 2.1 as the behavioral baseline for participation in this repository and its associated project spaces.

## Our standard

We expect participants to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good faith while remaining willing to correct misunderstandings.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, implementation, and behavior rather than people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and confidentiality.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks.

The standard applies to issues, pull requests, code review, discussions, documentation, project communication, and other spaces used for Tinlance project participation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment or discriminatory conduct, sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention, trolling, insults or personal attacks, publishing another person's private information without permission, threats or encouragement of violence, deliberate disruption of project collaboration, and other conduct that would reasonably make the project unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Reporting

For a private conduct concern, email **hello@tinlance.com** with the subject:

CODE OF CONDUCT: <short description>

Do not use a public issue for a sensitive conduct report.

The current enforcement contact is **@LloydCoder**.

## Enforcement

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

Tinlance targets acknowledgement within **3 business days**. The maintainer may:

1. Take no action when the report is unsupported or outside scope.
2. Request clarification or additional evidence.
3. Provide a private warning.
4. Temporarily restrict participation.
5. Remove content or access where appropriate.
6. Permanently restrict participation for serious or repeated violations.
7. Escalate matters to appropriate authorities where required by law.

The response will be proportionate to the circumstances, evidence, history, and impact. Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Privacy

People handling conduct reports will respect the privacy of reporters and subjects as far as practical and lawful. Information will be shared only with people who need it to investigate or respond.
