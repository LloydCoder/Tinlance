# Content Governance

Status: `CURRENT_IMPLEMENTED`

## Publishing gate

A public technical article, research item or case study must have:

1. accurate claims;
2. identifiable author/source provenance;
3. evidence for material technical claims;
4. methodology where the content reports an experiment;
5. explicit limitations where evidence is bounded;
6. security review for sensitive material;
7. a stable canonical URL;
8. appropriate internal links and a contextual CTA;
9. valid metadata and structured data where applicable.

## Technical review

Technical claims are reviewed by the responsible Tinlance engineering owner before publication. Research is not treated as production validation unless the evidence actually supports that conclusion.

## AI-assisted content

AI may assist with research organization, outlines, summarization and editing. Human technical ownership remains mandatory. Tinlance will not use automated generation to create thin keyword permutations, fabricated research, testimonials, statistics or case studies.

## Corrections

Material factual errors are corrected in the source document. `updatedAt` is changed only when the content is substantively revised. If a historical result is superseded, the original context remains identifiable and the replacement explains the change.

## Retractions

A finding that is invalidated or materially misleading is marked `DEPRECATED` or otherwise withdrawn from indexable authority surfaces as appropriate. The reason and replacement evidence should be documented rather than silently rewriting history.

## Security publication review

Never publish secrets, credentials, tenant data, private endpoints, unpublished vulnerability details, private customer architecture or production configuration. Public research must be sanitized before publication.

## Evidence levels

Case-study claims use: `CLIENT-PROVIDED`, `TINLANCE-MEASURED`, `EXTERNALLY-VERIFIED`, `HISTORICAL`, `ESTIMATED`, or `QUALITATIVE`.

Estimates and qualitative feedback must never be presented as measured outcomes.
