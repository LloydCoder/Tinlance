# Research Policy

Status: `CURRENT_IMPLEMENTED`

## Research classes

Tinlance distinguishes:

- original Tinlance research;
- external research synthesis;
- historical project evidence;
- opinion or interpretation.

Original experiments must identify methodology, dataset/environment, result and limitations. External claims should prefer primary sources.

## ThreatFade evidence boundary

The historical ThreatFade MVP evidence remains public:

- 490,847 packets;
- real Merlin QUIC C2 traffic;
- z-score 14.76;
- 0% false positives across the tested MVP populations.

These are historical verified experiment results. They are not a universal production guarantee, current engine benchmark, enterprise false-positive rate, or certification.

## Versioning

Research is treated as an evidence record. Substantive updates change `updatedAt` and should explain what changed. Invalidated findings are superseded or deprecated rather than silently rewritten.

## Reproducibility

Where a dataset, code or methodology can be public without exposing sensitive material, provide references. When evidence is proprietary or cannot be fully disclosed, state that limitation explicitly. Never fabricate missing methodology.

## Security

Public research must not disclose secrets, credentials, tenant data, private architecture, internal endpoints, or unpublished vulnerability information. Security findings follow responsible disclosure obligations before publication.
